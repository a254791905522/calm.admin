CalmAdmin

Everyday admin, made clearer

Free Utilities iPhone & iPad 4+

Organize bills, forms, notices, and paperwork in one calm place. CalmAdmin helps you see what each item is, what matters most, what to do next, what may still be missing, and whether it can be archived when you are done—so everyday admin feels less stressful and easier to act on.

What you can do

  • Import PDFs, scans, screenshots, and photos from Files, Photos, or the share sheet.
  • Open items in a clean viewer built for quick reading and low-stress action.
  • Use OCR and text extraction when possible to search and understand documents.
  • Search by title, text, issuer, amount, due date, category, or note.
  • Track status—active, paid, signed, sent, waiting, done, or archived.
  • Set reminders for payment, signature, reply, submission, or follow-up.

Next Step Decoder

Each item becomes a clear next step: pay, sign, reply, keep as proof, or notice what is missing before you act. A simple Calm Score overview shows how under control your admin feels—without judgment—so paperwork stays visible and manageable.

Built for real life

  • Human-friendly categories: Needs Action, To Pay, To Sign, To Reply, To Submit, To Keep, Missing Documents, Waiting On, Archived.
  • Designed for home admin, family paperwork, reimbursements, insurance, and small tasks that pile up.
  • Core experience is fully free—essential features are not locked behind a paywall.
